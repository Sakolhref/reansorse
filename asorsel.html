$$ q = Ze $$

ដោយ:

  • $$ ( q ) = charge (Coulombs) $$
  • $$ ( Z ) = atomic\ number, total\ protons\ in\ a\ nucleus $$
  • $$ ( e ) = 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \, C $$
